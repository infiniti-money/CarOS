# Safety and location sharing
Manual SOS opens the system dialer after explicit confirmation of a user-entered valid number. No emergency call, SMS, auto dispatch, or emergency service guarantee. One-shot GPS requests foreground permission, rejects stale or inaccurate fixes and shows a preview. Explicit copy rechecks freshness; recipient and send are chosen outside CarOS. Clipboard is not revocable after another app reads it; no automatic send, location stream, background access or persistence. Preview is cleared on background or cancellation. Validate real permissions, phone dialer and clipboard on devices.
