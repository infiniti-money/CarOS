# Privacy and local records
Settings opens PrivacyScreen. No category selected by default. Explicit selection, preview and native share for trip history, precise saved parking, and playback title/position (never URL). Export only known validated fields. Explicit confirmed deletion targets only selected keys, reports failures, does not clear preferences or protected videos. Stop active trip/playback before deletion: future activity can create new records. Share sheet cannot guarantee delivery or revoke previously shared data. Open OS app settings for permissions; no background permission requests. Dash Cam videos managed separately; this is not an all-device wipe or complete privacy audit. Test permission denial/revocation, system share sheet and real device behavior.
